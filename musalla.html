Local Masjids

Nearby masjids, for Jumu'ah, taraweeh, or just a quieter place to pray. Sorted by distance from UTM.

Jumu'ah khutbah times shift a few times a year as sunset changes, so we're not printing exact clock times here — they'd go stale. Tap "Get Directions" or call ahead for this week's confirmed time before you head out.

Iqbal Musallah

4099 Erin Mills Pkwy

~2.0 km

A small, informal musallah inside a grocery store — handy for a quick prayer between errands, not a full Jumu'ah venue.

Get Directions 905-820-3300

Shalimar Islamic Centre

3024 Cedarglen Gate, Mississauga

~2.0 km

Community masjid in a quiet residential area — five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, and weekly youth/Quran programs.

Get Directions 647-920-3024

ICCO — MAC Masjid

2550 Dunwin Dr, Mississauga

~2.6 km

Islamic Community Centre of Ontario — a large, well-equipped centre with a gym, youth programs, and full facilities for men, women, and families.

Get Directions 289-430-5171

ISNA Canada — Islamic Centre

2200 S Sheridan Way, Mississauga

~3.3 km

One of the GTA's largest, longest-running Islamic centres — sets the standard prayer timetable most Toronto-area masjids follow. Home to ISNA Cares as well.

Get Directions 905-403-8406

Muslim Welfare Center — Mussalah Al-Abbas

3490 Mavis Rd, Mississauga

~3.3 km

Small but busy neighbourhood musallah, attached to a community food bank — a good backup option when other spots are full.

Get Directions 905-281-9730
