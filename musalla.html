Prayer rooms

Brothers' and Sisters' prayer rooms are open daily for all five prayers. Ask any exec or check our Instagram for exact room locations this term.

Jumu'ah on campus

Weekly Jumu'ah logistics (space, khateeb, and schedule) are arranged by our Prayer Services Directors each term. Details will be posted here once confirmed for this term.

Local masjids

Nearby masjids for Jumu'ah, taraweeh, or a quieter place to pray, sorted by distance from UTM.

Jumu'ah khutbah times shift a few times a year as sunset changes, so we don't print exact times here. Call ahead or check the masjid's own listing for this week's time before you head out.

  • Iqbal Musallah

    4099 Erin Mills Pkwy, Mississauga

    About 2.0 km away

    A small, informal musallah inside a grocery store. Handy for a quick prayer between errands, but not a full Jumu'ah venue.

    Get directions to Iqbal Musallah (opens in a new tab) Call 905-820-3300 for Iqbal Musallah

  • Shalimar Islamic Centre

    3024 Cedarglen Gate, Mississauga

    About 2.0 km away

    Community masjid in a quiet residential area, with five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, and weekly youth and Quran programs.

    Get directions to Shalimar Islamic Centre (opens in a new tab) Call 647-920-3024 for Shalimar Islamic Centre

  • ICCO: MAC Masjid

    2550 Dunwin Dr, Mississauga

    About 2.6 km away

    Islamic Community Centre of Ontario. A large, well-equipped centre with a gym, youth programs, and full facilities for men, women, and families.

    Get directions to ICCO: MAC Masjid (opens in a new tab) Call 289-430-5171 for ICCO: MAC Masjid

  • ISNA Canada Islamic Centre

    2200 S Sheridan Way, Mississauga

    About 3.3 km away

    One of the GTA's largest and longest-running Islamic centres. It sets the standard prayer timetable most Toronto-area masjids follow, and is home to ISNA Cares.

    Get directions to ISNA Canada Islamic Centre (opens in a new tab) Call 905-403-8406 for ISNA Canada Islamic Centre

  • Muslim Welfare Center: Mussalah Al-Abbas

    3490 Mavis Rd, Mississauga

    About 3.3 km away

    Small but busy neighbourhood musallah attached to a community food bank. A good backup when other spots are full.

    Get directions to Muslim Welfare Center: Mussalah Al-Abbas (opens in a new tab) Call 905-281-9730 for Muslim Welfare Center: Mussalah Al-Abbas
