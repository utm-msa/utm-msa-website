Mental Health

You don't have to carry it alone

University is a lot, and it's okay to need support. Whether it's a rough week or something heavier, here's where to turn — on campus and within the community.

Need help right now?

If you're in crisis or worried about your safety, please reach out immediately — any of these are free and confidential.

On Campus, Right Now UTM Campus Safety — 905-569-4333 24/7. Call if you or someone nearby needs urgent, in-person help.
Canada-Wide Crisis Line 988 — Call or Text Free, 24/7 Suicide Crisis Helpline, anywhere in Canada.
Student Helpline Good2Talk — 1-866-925-5454 24/7 confidential support for Ontario post-secondary students.

Local Masjids

Prayer spaces beyond campus

Nearby masjids, for Jumu'ah, taraweeh, or just a quieter place to pray. Sorted by distance from UTM.

Jumu'ah khutbah times shift a few times a year as sunset changes, so we're not printing exact clock times here — they'd go stale. Tap "Visit Website" on any masjid below for this week's confirmed time before you head out.

Iqbal Musallah

4099 Erin Mills Pkwy

~2.0 km

A small, informal musallah inside a grocery store — handy for a quick prayer between errands, not a full Jumu'ah venue.

Get Directions 905-820-3300

Shalimar Islamic Centre

3024 Cedarglen Gate, Mississauga

~2.0 km

Community masjid in a quiet residential area — five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, and weekly youth/Quran programs.

Get Directions 647-920-3024

ICCO — MAC Masjid

2550 Dunwin Dr, Mississauga

~2.6 km

Islamic Community Centre of Ontario — a large, well-equipped centre with a gym, youth programs, and full facilities for men, women, and families.

Get Directions 289-430-5171

ISNA Canada — Islamic Centre

2200 S Sheridan Way, Mississauga

~3.3 km

One of the GTA's largest, longest-running Islamic centres — sets the standard prayer timetable most Toronto-area masjids follow. Home to ISNA Cares as well.

Get Directions 905-403-8406

Muslim Welfare Center — Mussalah Al-Abbas

3490 Mavis Rd, Mississauga

~3.3 km

Small but busy neighbourhood musallah, attached to a community food bank — a good backup option when other spots are full.

Get Directions 905-281-9730
Halal Food

Where to eat, on campus and nearby

On-campus dining and the closest verified halal spots — everything below is within about 3 km of UTM.

On Campus

UTM Dining (Dana Hospitality)

All chicken served at non-branded stations across campus is Halal-certified. UTM Dining publishes its supplier halal certificates and Dana Hospitality's team can help you pick suitable options — just ask, or email ahead if you have specific dietary needs.
