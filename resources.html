Mental Health

You don't have to carry it alone

University is a lot, and it's okay to need support. Whether it's a rough week or something heavier, here's where to turn, on campus and within the community.

Need help right now?

If you're in crisis or worried about your safety, reach out now. All of these are free and confidential, and they're open 24/7.

  • On campus, right now UTM Campus Safety Call 905-569-4333 for UTM Campus Safety Call if you or someone nearby needs urgent, in-person help.
  • Canada-wide crisis line 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline Call 988Text 988 Free, anywhere in Canada.
  • Student helpline Good2Talk Call 1-866-925-5454 for Good2Talk Confidential support for Ontario post-secondary students.

Ongoing support on campus

  • UTM Health & Counselling Centre

    905-828-5255

    Personal counselling, group counselling, and psychiatric care, with same-day and pre-booked appointments for all students.

    Where: Room DV 1152, William G. Davis BuildingEmail: health.utm@utoronto.ca

Academic & Financial Support

Help with school, and help making it work financially

Grades and money worries have a way of piling on at the same time. Here's what's around to make both a little lighter.

Academic support

  • Robert Gillespie Academic Skills Centre

    905-828-3858

    One-on-one appointments for writing, math, and study strategy, plus Facilitated Study Groups for first-year courses.

    Where: Room 3251, Maanjiwe nendamowinanEmail: academicskills.utm@utoronto.ca

    Visit website for the Academic Skills Centre (opens in a new tab)

  • UTM Library: Research Help

    905-828-5236

    One-on-one reference consultations and workshops on research tools, in the Hazel McCallion Academic Learning Centre.

    Visit website for the UTM Library (opens in a new tab)

  • MSA Study Groups

    Details coming

    Casual, community-run study sessions during exam season. Signup details will be posted here once they're scheduled.

Financial support

  • UTAPS

    University of Toronto Advance Planning for Students

    U of T's largest need-based grant program. It covers the gap between your actual costs and what government aid provides, and it doesn't need to be repaid.

    Learn more about UTAPS (opens in a new tab)

  • OSAP

    Ontario Student Assistance Program

    Loans, grants, and bursaries for eligible Ontario residents to help with tuition and living costs.

    Learn more about OSAP (opens in a new tab)

  • Zakat contact

    Details coming

    A confidential MSA contact for zakat-eligible students who need support. Contact details will be posted here once set up.

Halal Food

Where to eat, on campus and nearby

On-campus dining and the closest halal spots, all within about 3 km of UTM.

On campus

  • UTM Dining (Dana Hospitality)

    All chicken served at non-branded stations across campus is halal-certified. UTM Dining publishes its supplier halal certificates, and Dana Hospitality's team can help you pick suitable options. Just ask, or email ahead if you have specific dietary needs.

    View halal certificates for UTM Dining (opens in a new tab)Email Dana Hospitality

Off campus, within about 3 km

  • Monasaba

    Yemeni and Mediterranean

    About 2.0 km away

    Mandi and mixed grill platters. A longtime student favourite in the Dundas and Erin Mills plaza.

    Get directions to Monasaba (opens in a new tab)

  • Eat Meat BBQ

    Turkish and Middle Eastern BBQ

    About 2.0 km away

    Smoked meats and mixed grill platters, in the same plaza as Monasaba.

    Get directions to Eat Meat BBQ (opens in a new tab)

  • Original Shawarma

    Middle Eastern

    About 3.1 km away

    Halal burgers

    About 3.2 km away

    Get directions to Habibz Corner (opens in a new tab)

    Pakistani
