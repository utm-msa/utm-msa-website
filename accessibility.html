Accessibility

We want every student to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnifiers, or other assistive tools.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across the whole site, and to go beyond it where we can. We check pages with automated tools, keyboard-only testing, and feedback from real users.

Adjust the site for you

Select Display settings at the top of any page to change:

  • Text size: Default, Large, or Largest. Your browser's own zoom and text size settings also work on every page.
  • Contrast: High contrast makes all text near-white and every outline brighter.
  • Font: The easy-read option uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for people with low vision.
  • Text spacing: Wide spacing adds room between letters, words, and lines.
  • Motion: Reduce motion turns off animations. The site also follows your device's reduced motion setting automatically.

Your choices are saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

What we've built in

  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • A clear gold focus outline wherever your keyboard is, on every link, button, and embedded post.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 everywhere, and 7 to 1 for almost all body text.
  • No text smaller than 14 pixels, and body text at 17 pixels.
  • Headings in a logical order, labelled page regions, and descriptive link text.
  • Tabs and pop-up details that work with a keyboard and announce themselves to screen readers.
  • Phone numbers you can tap to call, including our crisis lines.
  • Two ways to reach every page: the main menu and the site map in the footer.

Known limitations

  • Instagram and YouTube embeds are provided by those companies, and we can't fully control how they work with assistive technology. Every embed has a plain link to the original, and you can skip past the Instagram post.
  • Lecture captions come from YouTube. We turn captions on by default, but some older videos only have automatic captions.
  • Prayer times and events load from outside services. If they can't load, the page tells you and points you to another source.

Tell us what isn't working

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you need information in a different format, email us at msa@utmsu.ca with the subject "Website accessibility". Tell us which page you were on and what happened. We especially welcome feedback from students who use screen readers or other assistive technology.
