Who we are

Our purpose is to serve the best interests of Muslims at the University of Toronto Mississauga, support our members, and give every student on campus, Muslim or not, a genuine way to engage with the Muslims around them.

    Getting involved

    There's a place for you here, whether you want to show up to events, lend a hand now and then, or help run things.

    • Become a general member

      Any UTM student can join. General members get our newsletter, can volunteer at events, and are part of the community the MSA exists to serve. It's free and takes a minute.

      Become a General Member (opens in a new tab)

    • Volunteer at an event

      Our events run on volunteers, from setup to sponsorship to clean-up. Sign up as a general member and our volunteer coordinators will reach out when help is needed.

      See what's coming up

    Our departments

    The work is split across these departments. Each one is a place you could help out.

    • Student LifeBrothers' and Sisters' Events, Islamic Education, and Volunteer Coordination
    • Internal AffairsIslamic Awareness, Brothers' and Sisters' Prayer Services, and Marketing (Content Creation and Graphic Design)
    • Public AffairsCampus Advocacy and International Advocacy
    • FinanceCharity and Partnerships

    Code of conduct

    Everyone at an MSA event or in an MSA space should feel safe, respected, and welcome. Our code of conduct sets out what we expect from members, volunteers, and the team, and what happens if those expectations aren't met.

    Read the UTM MSA Code of Conduct (opens in a new tab)

    If something happened at one of our events or spaces that didn't sit right with you, you can tell us in confidence at msa@utmsu.ca. If you are in danger or need urgent help on campus, call UTM Campus Safety at 905-569-4333.
